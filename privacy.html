Privacy Policy

Effective 07-03-2026 · AA Premier Roofing & Construction (AA Consulting TX)

What we collect

When you request an inspection or contact us through this site, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name, phone number, email address, property address or city, the service you are interested in, your preferred day and time, and anything you write in the message field.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request and schedule your inspection or project.
  • To prepare estimates and, if you hire us, to perform the work you request.
  • To keep basic records of jobs and communication, as any contractor does.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share it with third parties for their marketing.
  • We do not send you marketing you did not ask for.

Your information may pass through the service providers that run this website and our scheduling and customer-management tools. Those providers process it on our behalf only.

Insurance claims

If we assist with a storm or hail insurance claim at your request, we share job documentation (photos, measurements, damage reports) with your insurance company as part of that claim. We only do this for your claim and with your knowledge.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Email aaconsultingtx@gmail.com or call (940) 783-2238 and we will take care of it.

Changes

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
