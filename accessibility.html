Accessibility Statement

Updated 07-03-2026 · AA Premier Roofing & Construction

We want every homeowner to be able to use this site, including people who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, or reduced-motion settings. We treat our clients like family, and that includes how we build our website.

What this site does

  • Text alternatives on meaningful images and icons.
  • Full keyboard access to navigation, the comparison slider, and the contact form.
  • Honors your system's reduced-motion setting: animations are turned off and all content is shown directly.
  • Color contrast and readable type sizes checked across the site.
  • Proper page structure (landmarks and headings) for screen readers.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA wherever practical, and we keep improving as the site grows.

Found a problem?

If any part of this site is hard for you to use, please tell us and we will fix it or help you directly. Email aaconsultingtx@gmail.com or call (940) 783-2238. You can always reach us by phone for anything the website does.
