Our standard

Integrity you can't see
from the street.

Anyone can lay shingles. A roof that survives Texas summers and hail seasons is built in layers, and every layer has to be done right. Here is what "premium" actually means on your home.

01 — Foundation

Sound structural decking

We start at the wood. Rotted or soft decking gets replaced and everything is properly fastened before a single shingle goes down. No shortcuts under the surface.

02 — Moisture barrier

Synthetic underlayment

A modern moisture barrier over the entire deck, protecting the wood even if wind-driven rain ever gets past the shingles. Lighter, tougher, and more water-resistant than old felt.

03 — Leak defense

Ice & water shield

Self-sealing membrane at the eaves and valleys, the exact spots where leaks start. This is the layer cut-rate roofers skip to save a few dollars. We don't.

04 — The surface

Premium architectural shingles

Heavy, dimensional shingles nailed to manufacturer specification, so your warranty is real and your roof holds its line against Texas wind and hail.

05 — Built to breathe

Ridge cap & ventilation

Sealed ridge caps and balanced attic ventilation that let heat and moisture escape, so your roof lasts longer and your energy bills stay lower through the summer.

Storm & hail damage

A free inspection costs you nothing. A missed problem can cost you everything.

North Texas takes some of the worst hail in the country. If a storm just rolled through, damage is often invisible from the ground and easy to miss until the next leak. We'll climb up, document everything, and handle the insurance side for you.

  • Free storm inspection

    From filing to the final nail, one crew handles the whole job.
